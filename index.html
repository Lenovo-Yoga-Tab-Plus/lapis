Linux on slot A. Android stays on slot B.

Mu-Silicium UEFI, GRUB, and an EndeavourOS root. The kernel is linux-lapis. The device packages are lapis-pkgs. The bootloader unlock image is built on your computer from the tablet’s serial.

Install guide Hardware
Unlock unlock-bootloader.py writes sn.img for the fastboot serial. Unlocking erases Android’s data. Partition From TWRP, shrink userdata and add a Linux partition named arch. The numbers belong to that disk. Flash slot A UEFI, EFI, kernel, and the root image. Slot B, userdata, and the signed vbmeta stay untouched.

On the tablet

Display and pen

The Tianma panel, touch, and the pen. The folio blanks the screen. It does not suspend.

Pogo keyboard

Keys and the clickpad work while the cover is attached.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

WCN7850. Bluetooth is unbound across suspend and comes back with the factory address.

USB network

Plugged into a host, the tablet is 172.16.42.1.

Cameras

Rear GC13A0 and front GC13A2.

Six speakers

The sound card name stays Lenovo-TB520FU. Internal microphones work.

Working

  • Panel, touch, pen, and folio blanking
  • Keyboard, clickpad, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and USB
  • Both cameras, the speakers, and the internal microphones
  • Light sensor and orientation from the accelerometer
  • Suspend, including with the USB cable unplugged

Still open

  • The compass. This tablet’s ADSP firmware has no magnetometer.
  • The proximity sensor does not produce a reading.
  • The headset microphone is configured and has not been tested with a plug.
  • A very short sound at the start of playback can clip.
